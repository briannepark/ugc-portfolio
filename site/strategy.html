Creative strategy

notes to self: what do people actually want to see?

My sketchbook of content ideas. Every one starts with a real question and a specific person.

1. find the real question
2. name the person asking it
3. answer it honestly, on camera
#1

Harvest to table

grow it, cook it,
eat it. one kitchen!

Can a countertop garden really make dinner?

Home cooks and parents who want fresher food without a yard

#2

The 30-day experiment

track it daily.
show the boring days too

Does it change anything you can actually measure?

Data-curious shoppers weighing a big purchase

#3

The day-90 skin journey

same light. same spot.
no filter.

What does skin look like at day 90, really?

Skincare skeptics tired of week-one miracles

#4

The label read-along

highlighter on
the ingredient list!

What's actually in this, and does it matter?

Ingredient-conscious shoppers and sensitive skin

#5

A day in the home

the toy-bin test:
does it survive?

Does it fit a real, messy day?

Busy parents who need things to just work

#6

Before you buy

one honest caveat
= trust

What do I wish I'd known first?

First-time buyers on the fence

if i wouldn't stop scrolling for it, i don't post it.
Have a product in mind?

Let's find the question
your customers are asking.
