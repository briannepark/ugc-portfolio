Why this stay

The feeling I'd film

The most "New York at night" stay on the list. It gives a hotel pitch its strongest opening shot: the window, then the skyline, then a walk that starts right at the lobby.

What it's known for
  • Oversize windows and gold-link light fixtures in the rooms.
  • José Andrés restaurants in the hotel.
  • Nubeluz, a cocktail bar on the 50th floor.
  • In NoMad, with Madison Square Park, Flatiron, and Chelsea nearby.
