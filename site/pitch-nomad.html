Highlights

Luxury, made easy for the whole family

A beautiful hotel is only worth it if it works for the people I travel with. Here is what makes this one easy with a baby and a dog.

For baby

  • Cribs, extra beds, adjoining rooms and kids' bath productsconfirm

    Are part of Ritz-Carlton's family offering. Ask for each when booking.

  • Bath timeconfirm

    Deep soaking tubs in select rooms. Request a tub room for the baby's bath.

  • 24-hour in-room dining,

    So bottle and snack times never depend on a restaurant's hours.

  • Childcareconfirm

    The concierge can arrange it for an evening out.

  • Ritz Kidsconfirm

    Is for ages 4 to 12, and Nubeluz has kids' options at weekend high tea (Sat and Sun, 11 to 3). Ask about this season's Ritz Kids tea.

For the dog

  • Pets welcomeconfirm

    Up to two, 60 lbs combined, no breed restrictions.

  • Pet beds, bowls and treatsconfirm

    Are provided, subject to availability.

  • Morning and afternoon walksconfirm

    Madison Square Park is a few minutes away, with Jemmy's Dog Run: separate small and large dog areas, open with the park (around 6 am to 11 pm), waste bags provided.

  • Relief breakconfirm

    The front desk can point to the closest grassy spot for a quick potty walk.

  • The feeconfirm

    Is per stay and reported anywhere from $250 to $375.

For mom

  • Fitness center

    Open 24 hours, with Peloton bikes, right by the spa.

  • Spaconfirm

    A 6,800 sq ft spa with a sauna and steam room.

  • Bath and rain shower

    Diptyque products in every room.

  • Nubeluz and Bazaar Meat

    Are in the building, so a date night is an elevator ride.

Why this stay

What I'd show other moms

I'm a mom who travels with a baby and a dog, and I want other moms to see what's possible. Pampering and excitement belong back in the picture, without giving up anything baby or the dog needs. This stay makes that real: quiet rooms for naps, soaking tubs for bath time, a gym downstairs, a skyline bar for the night, and the trendy pop-ups I usually miss a short ride away.
