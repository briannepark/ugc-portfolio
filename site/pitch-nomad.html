The trip

Picture this. You land in New York, ride into NoMad, and step into a room where the window is so wide the skyline feels hung just for you. This is the long weekend I'd take you on, one stop at a time.

Day 1 · Arrival

Through the doors

Walk in with me. Past the sheer curtains, toward the glow of the bar, and then up to a window with the whole city lit below. This is how I'd open the trip: moving through the hotel, one room at a time.
Step through the doors.

Sheer curtains, floor-to-ceiling windows, and a sculptural chandelier over the lounge.

Past the lounge, toward the bar.

Green velvet, brass, and a wall of hanging greenery behind the bottles.

Then up, to the view.

Dusk over the downtown skyline, with every light just switching on.

Why this stay

The feeling I'd film

The most "New York at night" stay on the list. It gives a hotel pitch its strongest opening shot: the window, then the skyline, then a walk that starts right at the lobby.
