A little about me

Curious by nature,
careful by habit.

By day I'm a software engineer: five-plus years of asking why something works, not just whether it does. That habit follows me home, which is how I became the person who reads the ingredient list before the ad copy.

At home I'm a mom who is always quietly testing something: a gentler detergent, a dish soap without the harsh stuff, a skincare routine my sensitive skin can actually live with. I keep what works and tell you the truth about what doesn't.

And somewhere in between I'm a creative, a plant person, and a proud Kanaka. All of it ends up in the work, which is why you'll find real routines and real rooms here, and nothing staged to look like more than it is.
